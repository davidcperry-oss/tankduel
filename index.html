Frequently asked questions

How do I play online?

Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then choose Find Opponent, Online Free-For-All or Online Downward Spiral from the main menu.

How do I control my tank?

Use the left pad to move and tap its centre to drop a mine. Use the right pad to aim and tap its centre to fire. Tap the ? button on the main menu for the full guide and power-up list.

How do I unlock Garage items?

Most items unlock by reaching goals shown under each item, such as wins, hits or walls destroyed. Limited-time seasonal items are free to claim while they're available: equip one during its season to keep it.

A new seasonal item isn't showing up

New items download automatically. Make sure you're online, then close and reopen the Garage. It can take up to an hour to appear.

How do I turn off notifications?

Open the Settings app, choose Notifications, then TankUNext.
