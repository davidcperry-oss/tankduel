Tank Duel Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Tank Duel is made by People Capital Management LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. In short: we don't collect, store or sell any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Tank Duel has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Information stored on your device

Your settings, Garage selections, loadouts, call sign and lifetime stats are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Game Center

Online matches, leaderboards and achievements use Apple's Game Center. When you play online, your Game Center name or chosen call sign and your tank's look are shared with the other players in that match so they can see you. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive or store this information.

Downloadable Garage items

The app downloads new Garage items from Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database. This is a read-only download of game content and does not send us any personal information.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules alerts on your device about new Garage items. You can turn them off at any time in Settings.

Children

Tank Duel does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
